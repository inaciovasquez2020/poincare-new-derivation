# Minimal Missing Ingredients

## Lemma A — Local Spherical Descent
For every closed, connected, simply connected triangulated \(3\)-manifold \(T\) not PL-isomorphic to \(\partial\Delta^4\), there exists a finite sequence of homeomorphism-preserving local moves
\[
T=T_0\rightsquigarrow T_1\rightsquigarrow\cdots\rightsquigarrow T_m
\]
such that for some nonnegative integer-valued invariant \(\Phi\),
\[
\Phi(T_m)<\Phi(T).
\]

## Lemma B — Edge Imbalance
For every \(T\) with
\[
\Phi(T)>0,
\]
there exists an admissible \((3\to 2)\) site with strictly positive local descent.

## Lemma C — Zero-Defect Characterization
\[
\Phi(T)=0 \Rightarrow T\cong \partial\Delta^4.
\]

## Lemma D — Termination
Any strictly descending admissible sequence for \(\Phi\) terminates in finitely many steps.
