# G2 radial-chain decomposition and the parity bridge

**Status (2026-11-17): PAPER PROOF under the stated combinatorial PL-manifold assumptions. Lean theorem NOT_RUN.** This is a new, narrowly scoped proof obligation for PR #199. The independent global Poincaré derivation remains OPEN.

## 1. Precise combinatorial statement

Let `K` be a finite simplicial triangulation of a closed PL 3-manifold. Let `D` be the subcomplex of the barycentric subdivision generated by chains of nonempty primal faces of dimensions 1, 2 and 3. Its 2-simplices are flags `[e,f,T]` where `e ⊂ f ⊂ T` have dimensions 1, 2 and 3. Work throughout with simplicial chains over `F₂`.

Let `R` be the set of **radial edges** `[e,f]` and `[e,T]`. Let `Q` be the remaining edges `[f,T]`. For a primal edge `e`, put

`C_e := Σ_{e ⊂ f ⊂ T} [e,f,T] ∈ C₂(D;F₂).`

**Theorem (radial kernel).** For any `c ∈ C₂(D;F₂)`,

`(∂c)|_R = 0  ⇔  ∃! λ : K₁ → F₂, c = Σ_e λ(e) C_e.`

In particular, if `z ∈ C₁(D;F₂)` is supported on `Q` and `z=∂c`, then

`z = Σ_e λ(e) ∂C_e.`

No geometric disk homeomorphism or carrier comparison is used in this theorem.

## 2. Proof with all incidence cases

Write `c = Σ_{e⊂f⊂T} a(e,f,T) [e,f,T]`.

* For each `e ⊂ f`, the coefficient of the radial edge `[e,f]` in `∂c` is `a(e,f,T₀)+a(e,f,T₁)`, where `T₀,T₁` are the **two distinct** tetrahedra incident to the face `f`. Vanishing implies the two coefficients are equal.
* For each `e ⊂ T`, precisely two triangular faces `f₀,f₁` of `T` contain `e`. The coefficient of `[e,T]` in `∂c` is `a(e,f₀,T)+a(e,f₁,T)`. Vanishing implies equality.
* For fixed `e`, the bipartite incidence graph with vertices the faces and tetrahedra containing `e`, and edges the flags `e ⊂ f ⊂ T`, is connected: it is the incidence subdivision of the link cycle of `e`. By propagation along adjacent flags, `a(e,f,T)` has one value `λ(e)` independent of `f,T`.

The triangular flags partition by their bottom edge `e`, so `c = Σ_e λ(e) C_e` and these `λ(e)` are unique. Conversely each radial edge in `∂C_e` occurs twice and cancels; all radial coefficients of the sum vanish.

**Hypothesis audit.** It is essential that the input is an actual *simplicial* closed PL 3-manifold: every face belongs to two distinct tetrahedra, every tetrahedron has exactly two faces containing a fixed edge, and the edge link is connected. A pseudomanifold with disconnected edge link does not satisfy the one-coefficient-per-edge conclusion. If the repository's `Tet` encoding permits duplicated vertex supports, the distinctness/exhaustiveness hypotheses must be checked at the use site.

## 3. Pairing with a primal two-cycle

For a primal mod-two 2-cycle `S`, choose, once per primal face `f` with cofaces `T₀,T₁`, one of the two dual half-edges `[f,T₀]`, `[f,T₁]`. Define a cochain `α` on `C₁(D)` by putting `S(f)` on that chosen half-edge and zero on the other, and zero on every radial edge. For the subdivided dual edge path `[T₀,f]+[f,T₁]`, the cochain evaluates to `S(f)`, independent of choice.

Each whole fan chain `C_e` has outer boundary consisting of the two half-edges for **each** face `f` containing `e` (once each); consequently

`⟨α,∂C_e⟩ = Σ_{f⊃e} S(f) = (∂S)(e) = 0.`

For any dual-walk 1-cycle `z` supported on the `[f,T]` edges, if `z=∂c` in `D`, the radial-kernel theorem gives

`⟨α,z⟩ = Σ_e λ(e)⟨α,∂C_e⟩ = 0.`

Therefore a filling theorem for the **actual dual-walk cycles** in `D` yields the exact no-odd-return hypothesis required by the existing abstract `Poincare.DualPotentialCandidate.potential_of_no_odd_return`, once the graph-path-to-simplicial-chain interpretation and dual-graph connectivity are formalized.

**Important distinction:** `α` need **not** be a cocycle on every individual flag triangle. Its vanishing on complete fan boundaries, together with the radial-kernel decomposition, is the valid argument.

## 4. Remaining topological comparison

A route to `H₁(D;F₂)=0` without Poincaré classification is:

1. The barycentric-coordinate deletion of all original-vertex barycenters yields a deformation retraction `|sd K| \ K⁰ → |D|`. For a point outside `K⁰`, the sum of coefficients on vertices of dimensions 1,2,3 is positive; set the original-vertex barycentric coordinate to zero and renormalize. Linear interpolation within each simplex gives a deformation homotopy.
2. Removing finitely many points from a connected, simply connected 3-manifold preserves its fundamental group, by the local punctured 3-ball and van Kampen argument.
3. Hence `π₁(|D|)=0` and therefore `H₁(D;F₂)=0`, using the comparison of simplicial homology with the realization's singular homology.
