# Direct finite-rank proof of the G2 no-odd-return bridge

**Status:** PROVED on paper under explicit assumptions; Lean NOT_RUN, no axiom audit. This replaces the previous need for a separately formalized `H₁(D;F₂)=0` comparison **for the parity conclusion**. It does not prove global JIID descent or independent Poincaré.

## 1. Incidence setup

Let `K` be a finite **connected closed simplicial PL 3-manifold**, with actual sets of distinct primal vertices, edges, triangular faces, and tetrahedra. Write their cardinalities `V,E,F,T`. Work over `k=F₂`.

Let the dual multigraph `G*` have one vertex for each tetrahedron and one edge for each primal triangle, incident to its **two distinct** tetrahedron cofaces. Its boundary map `∂* : k^F → k^T` sends `f` to the sum of these two tetrahedra.

Define the primal edge-face coboundary

`Φ : k^E → k^F,   (Φ λ)(f) = Σ_{e⊂f} λ(e).`

An edge fan `C_e` of barycentric flag triangles has outer boundary the subdivided dual cycle corresponding to `Φ(1_e)`, because every face `f⊃e` contributes its two half-edges `[f,T₀]` and `[f,T₁]`. Thus `Φ` is exactly the **dual cell boundary-vector map** after contracting the valence-two face-barycenter subdivisions.

## 2. Image lies in the dual cycle space

For each tetrahedron `T`,

`(∂*Φ λ)(T) = Σ_{f⊂T}Σ_{e⊂f}λ(e)=0,`

because every edge of a 3-simplex belongs to exactly two triangular faces. Thus `im Φ ⊆ ker ∂* = Z₁(G*;k)`. This holds for any simplicial closed 3-pseudomanifold with the stated two-coface incidence.

## 3. Rank of Φ from simply connectedness

Let `δ₀:k^V→k^E` be `(δ₀ a)(uv)=a(u)+a(v)`. Then `Φ=δ₁`, the primal simplicial coboundary, and `Φ δ₀=0`. If `|K|` is simply connected, `H¹(K;k)=0` (via the simplicial/singular comparison and fundamental group, or directly by edge-path homotopy). Thus

`ker Φ = im δ₀.`

The primal 1-skeleton is connected, so `ker δ₀` consists of the two constant vertex labelings and has dimension 1. Therefore

`dim ker Φ = dim im δ₀ = V-1,`
`rank Φ=E-V+1.`

**Alternative finite proof target without cohomology:** A closed edge-cochain with zero sum around each triangular face determines a vertex potential by path integration. Its independence of path follows because the carrier is simply connected and edge-path homotopies are generated by backtrack and triangle-boundary insertions. This is the precise remaining topological premise if mathlib cohomology comparison is unavailable.

## 4. Counts from local manifold links

Four triangular faces belong to each tetrahedron and two tetrahedra to each triangular face, hence `F=2T`.

For each primal vertex `v`, the link is a triangulated 2-sphere and has Euler characteristic 2. Sum its Euler identity over all primal vertices. An edge is counted at its 2 endpoints, each face at its 3 vertices, and each tetrahedron at its 4 vertices:

`2V = 2E - 3F + 4T.`

With `F=2T`, this gives `E=V+T`, and therefore

`rank Φ=E-V+1=T+1.`

This derives the needed odd-dimensional Euler identity using only local links; no classification of the ambient 3-manifold is used.

The dual graph is connected (connected triangulated closed manifold, with tetrahedra joined through faces), has `T` vertices and `F=2T` edges, hence its mod-two cycle-space dimension is

`dim Z₁(G*;k) = F-T+1=T+1.`

Consequently `im Φ=Z₁(G*;k)` by inclusion and equal finite dimension.

## 5. No-odd-return for an arbitrary primal face 2-cycle

Let `S∈k^F` be a primal mod-two 2-cycle, so for each edge `e`:

`(∂₂ S)(e)=Σ_{f⊃e}S(f)=0.`

Let `z` be the dual-edge 1-chain of any closed dual graph walk, reduced modulo two. Because `z ∈ Z₁(G*;k)=im Φ`, choose `λ∈k^E` such that `z=Φλ`. Then

`Σ_f S(f)z(f) = Σ_f S(f)Σ_{e⊂f}λ(e) = Σ_e λ(e)Σ_{f⊃e}S(f)=0.`

This is exactly the parity of the lifted closed dual walk. For a connected dual graph, zero parity on every closed return implies the `hzero` hypothesis of `Poincare.DualPotentialCandidate.potential_of_no_odd_return`; path reachability supplies its `hpath` hypothesis. The resulting potential satisfies `S(f)=B(T₀)+B(T₁)`, giving `S=∂₃ B`.

No geometric disk embedding or simplicial filling in `D` is necessary for this algebraic route.

## 6. Quantitative obstruction beyond simply connectedness

For a connected closed simplicial PL 3-manifold without assuming `H¹=0`, let `b₁=dim_k H¹(K;k)`. The exact sequence `im δ₀⊆ker Φ` yields

`rank Φ=E-(V-1+b₁)=T+1-b₁.`

Since the dual graph cycle space always has dimension `T+1`, its quotient by edge-fan boundaries has dimension **exactly `b₁`**:

`dim_k [Z₁(G*;k)/im Φ] = b₁(K;k).`

Thus the failure of disk-boundary generation is measured precisely by first cohomology, not by an unspecified geometric obstruction. The original chain decomposition within `C₂(D)` remains unique by primal edge, but the representation of an *outer dual cycle* `z=Σ_e λ_e ∂C_e` is generally **not unique**: its kernel consists of primal vertex coboundaries when `b₁=0`.
