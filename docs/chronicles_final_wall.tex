\section{Hypergraph Final Wall (Chronicles)}

\textbf{Conditional Theorem.}
Fix $k,\Delta,R$ and a non-tree hypergraph gadget $H$. Assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Bounded-multiplicity cover reduction $G \mapsto \mathcal O_H(G)$ with overlap size $\le B$,
\item Overlap-packing: $\operatorname{cx}_H(G)\to\infty \Rightarrow m\to\infty$ controlled witnesses,
\item Non-collision lemma with constant $C=C(k,\Delta,R,H)$.
\end{enumerate}

Then:
\[
\#\{\operatorname{tp}^k_R(v)\} \;\ge\; C^{-1} m.
\]

Hence:
\[
\operatorname{cx}_H(G)\to\infty \;\Longrightarrow\; \#\{\operatorname{tp}^k_R(v)\}\to\infty.
\]

Contraposition:
\[
\#\{\operatorname{tp}^k_R(v)\}\le C_0
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\operatorname{cx}_H(G)\le C \cdot C_0.
\]

Thus:
\[
\exists C' = C'(k,\Delta,R,H)\ \text{s.t.}\ 
\#\{\operatorname{tp}^k_R(v)\}\le C'
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\operatorname{cx}_H(G)\le C'.
\]
